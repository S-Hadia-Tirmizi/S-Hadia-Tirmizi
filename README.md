# Syeda Hadia Tirmizi

### Biotechnology | Molecular Medicine | Computational Biology

My journey in biotechnology began with a curiosity about biological systems and the mechanisms that govern them. During my undergraduate years, that curiosity took me from molecular biology and laboratory investigations to a deeper interest in the questions hidden within biological data.

Working across experimental and computational settings gradually changed how I approached research. I became increasingly interested not only in observing biological phenomena, but also in understanding the patterns behind them, examining the evidence statistically, and exploring what those findings might reveal about disease biology.

Today, my research interests span molecular medicine, cancer biology, hematological research, computational genomics, and biomedical data analytics. I enjoy moving between biological questions and analytical methods, using statistical programming, bioinformatics platforms, molecular modeling tools, and scientific visualization to investigate problems from different perspectives.

My experience with R, Python, SPSS, and other scientific software has shaped my quantitative approach to research. Equally, my laboratory background has taught me to value experimental design, methodological precision, biological variability, and the importance of interpreting results within their scientific context.

I also enjoy teaching statistical and computational methods to medical students, particularly helping them understand the reasoning behind an analysis rather than simply following a sequence of commands.

### What I Explore

**Molecular Medicine and Disease Biology**

Understanding molecular mechanisms, disease-associated patterns, and potential therapeutic vulnerabilities.

**Biostatistics and Quantitative Research**

Statistical inference, regression, survival analysis, research design, and the interpretation of biomedical data.

**Computational Biology and Bioinformatics**

Transcriptomic analysis, pathway investigation, molecular modeling, and computational approaches to biological questions.

**Experimental Research**

Molecular biology, hematology, cell-based assays, and laboratory investigations.

### Beyond the Analysis

I believe meaningful research begins with asking the right question. The choice of method matters, but so do the assumptions behind it, the quality of the data, and the biological interpretation of the results.

This space brings together my research projects, analytical workflows, and scientific explorations. It is a record of the questions I investigate, the methods I develop, and the way I continue to grow as a researcher.
